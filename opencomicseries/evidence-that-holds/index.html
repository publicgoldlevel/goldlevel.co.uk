Evidence That Holds

OCS-2026-001·series·10 ordered platesPrimary series
Plate 1 of 10

Summary

A pure comic expression of evidence discipline. The sequence follows the ways a record can be narrowed, echoed, inflated, pressured, and closed before it has been tested well enough to hold.

What it maps

Selective case-building, asymmetric selection, echoed authority, crowd and institution pressure, story and cause traps, numerical overreach, context and language fog, attachment, escalation, and premature closure.

Why it matters

Public reasoning becomes more trustworthy when claims remain inspectable, contextual, revisable, and proportionate to the evidence. Comic form makes those distortions visible without requiring the reader to adopt a specialist vocabulary first.

This is an editorial interpretation of the ordered visual work. The sequence is presented once, in authored order, without duplicated cover frames.
