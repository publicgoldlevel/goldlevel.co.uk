RELATIONSHIP PRESSURE — PAUSE, NAME, OWN, PROTECT AGENCY

FA-2026-022·series·7 ordered frames·No. 14 in field

Summary

A practical relationship process that moves from activation to proportionate action. The sequence pauses reaction, identifies what is happening, separates ownership from over-carrying, and keeps both people’s choices visible. The sequence links pause, pattern naming, and ownership without collapsing their differences.

What it maps

Pause, pattern naming, ownership, boundaries, agency, proportionate response, and review of what changes after the immediate pressure has passed. The sequence also shows where pause and pattern naming align, conflict, or require separation.

Why it matters

Pressure can make urgency feel like truth and responsibility feel one-sided. A clear sequence interrupts escalation and creates a better chance of honest communication, repair, or a dignified boundary. The result is a stronger basis for deciding what pause and pattern naming require.
