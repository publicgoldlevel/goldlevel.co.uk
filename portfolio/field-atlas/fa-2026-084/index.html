UNION THROUGH PRAYER

FA-2026-084·series·7 ordered frames·No. 11 in field
Frame 1 of 7

Summary

A symbolic and devotional sequence exploring prayer as a relational act that gathers attention, care, and purpose. It presents covering, calling, and restoration as themes of union rather than as externally verified spiritual claims. The work keeps prayer, union, and covering close to consequence.

What it maps

Prayer, union, covering, calling, restoration, devotion, relationship, attention, and the symbolic movement from separation towards gathered presence. The field becomes clearer where prayer and union change pace, role, or consequence.

Why it matters

Spiritual imagery can support reflection and shared meaning when its status remains clear. This work offers a contemplative route without claiming authority over another person’s belief or experience. That visibility helps people protect what matters when prayer and union interact.
