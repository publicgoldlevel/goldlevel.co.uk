Stewardship, Boundaries & Source Integrity

SIMPLE DISTINCTIONS HAVE TO REMAIN

FA-2026-051·standalone·1 frame·No. 10 in field
Frame 1 of 1

Summary

A compact statement on preserving distinctions that protect meaning. The work resists collapsing tool and person, output and proof, care and control, interpretation and fact, or convenience and legitimate authority. Its practical centre lies where category boundaries, language, and proof meet.

What it maps

Category boundaries, language, proof, role, personhood, authority, interpretation, and the pressure to make unlike things appear equivalent. It also attends to the boundaries that determine whether category boundaries or language carries authority.

Why it matters

When foundational distinctions disappear, accountability becomes difficult to locate and correction becomes harder to perform. Keeping them visible supports clearer judgement without denying complexity. It creates a clearer route for questioning how category boundaries and language are being carried.
