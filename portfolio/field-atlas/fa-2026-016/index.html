Relational Maturity

CHOOSE THE RIGHT RELATIONSHIP LENS

FA-2026-016·standalone·1 frame
Frame 1 of 1

Summary

A single-page overview reminding readers that similar distress can arise from different relational mechanisms. The next step depends on whether the issue is unsafe pressure, avoidance, unmet reassurance, boundary confusion, or an ending that needs clarity.

What it maps

Problem framing, attachment, avoidance, boundaries, reassurance, exit decisions, and the risk of applying one familiar explanation to every relationship difficulty. It also tracks how problem framing and attachment reinforce or constrain one another over time.

Why it matters

A wrong lens can produce a wrong intervention. Distinguishing the pattern before acting protects both people from unnecessary blame, misplaced repair attempts, or prolonged exposure to a situation that requires distance. That clarity supports more deliberate choices about problem framing and attachment in practice.

This is an editorial interpretation of the ordered visual work. It is not legal, clinical, audit, certification, or control-effectiveness advice.
