MISREAD AND PROJECTION

FA-2026-079·series·6 ordered frames
Frame 1 of 6

Summary

A visual sequence on misreading and projection in communication. It examines how another person’s fear, expectation, memory, or narrative can overwrite the speaker’s stated meaning and make correction difficult. Its centre is the interaction between speech, interpretation, and projection.

What it maps

Speech, interpretation, projection, misreading, argument, identity, memory, expectation, correction, and the difference between receiving a message and imposing a story. It keeps the transition between speech and interpretation visible, especially under pressure.

Why it matters

Repeated misreading can erode self-trust and make communication feel impossible. Naming the mechanism supports clearer boundaries, better checking, and a fairer distinction between impact and invented intent. It helps turn concern about speech and interpretation into a more accountable next step.

This is an editorial interpretation of the ordered visual work. It is not legal, clinical, audit, certification, or control-effectiveness advice.
