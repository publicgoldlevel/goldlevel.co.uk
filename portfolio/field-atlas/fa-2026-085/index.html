LIMEN — THE LIVE THRESHOLD BEFORE SELECTION

FA-2026-085·series·5 ordered frames
Frame 1 of 5

Summary

A linguistic and symbolic exploration of the Latin limen: threshold, sill, doorway, and entrance. The work holds the unstable edge before a thing has fully crossed into a selected category. It places threshold, entry, and outside and inside inside a shared decision field.

What it maps

Threshold, entry, outside and inside, liminality, selection, naming, classification, translation, observer, and the loss introduced by choosing one English equivalent. It includes the feedback loops through which threshold changes the practical meaning of entry.

Why it matters

Many decisions are shaped before formal selection appears to begin. Attending to the threshold makes preclassification pressures visible and protects ambiguity from being prematurely closed. It supports proportionate action by keeping threshold and entry open to review.

This is an editorial interpretation of the ordered visual work. It is not legal, clinical, audit, certification, or control-effectiveness advice.
