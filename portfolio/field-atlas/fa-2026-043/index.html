Relational Maturity & Repair

TELL THE TRUTH. BE KIND. CARRY YOUR PART.

FA-2026-043·series·3 ordered frames·No. 02 in fieldPrimary work
Frame 1 of 3

Summary

A compact behavioural standard for difficult situations. Truth without kindness can become cruelty, kindness without truth can become avoidance, and responsibility disappears when each person carries only the part that flatters them. Its practical centre lies where honesty, kindness, and ownership meet.

What it maps

Honesty, kindness, ownership, impact, boundaries, difficult conversation, and the balance required to keep care from erasing accountability. It also attends to the boundaries that determine whether honesty or kindness carries authority.

Why it matters

Simple principles are most useful under pressure. This triad offers a memorable check for communication and repair while leaving room for context, limits, and the other person’s agency. It creates a clearer route for questioning how honesty and kindness are being carried.
