Relational Maturity & Repair

GREED — FROM SCARCITY AND CONTROL TO SUFFICIENCY

FA-2026-061·series·5 ordered frames·No. 36 in field
Frame 1 of 5

Summary

A non-shaming reflection on greed as more than wanting. It traces how unmet need and fear can organise control before enoughness is recognised, then points towards grounded repair and responsible use. It places need, fear, and scarcity inside a shared decision field.

What it maps

Need, fear, scarcity, accumulation, control, sufficiency, value, stewardship, generosity, recognition, and the transition from compulsive more to right proportion. It includes the feedback loops through which need changes the practical meaning of fear.

Why it matters

Moral condemnation can hide the mechanism that needs change. Seeing the pull makes it easier to build conditions of sufficiency and choose generosity without denying real material need. It supports proportionate action by keeping need and fear open to review.
