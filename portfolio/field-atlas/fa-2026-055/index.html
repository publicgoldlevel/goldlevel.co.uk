WORDS SHAPE WORLDS

FA-2026-055·series·7 ordered frames·No. 06 in field
Frame 1 of 7

Summary

A symbolic sequence tracing how words become worlds through repeated stories and structures. It holds both creative and coercive possibilities, then returns interpretation to truth, action, coherence, and the continuing human presence. Its centre is the interaction between language, story, and structure.

What it maps

Language, story, structure, trust, solitude, shadow, guidance, capture, interpretation, and the return from symbolic world-building to human judgement. It keeps the transition between language and story visible, especially under pressure.

Why it matters

Words organise attention before they organise institutions. Seeing their double capacity helps readers use language deliberately and resist narratives that turn partial frames into total environments. It helps turn concern about language and story into a more accountable next step.
