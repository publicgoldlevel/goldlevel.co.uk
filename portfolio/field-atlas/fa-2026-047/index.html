WHICH DIRECTION? ALIGNMENT THROUGH DAILY DESIGN

FA-2026-047·series·3 ordered frames·No. 09 in field
Frame 1 of 3

Summary

A visual sequence asking how direction becomes embodied in ordinary practice. It treats daily design choices as the place where values meet schedules, systems, environments, habits, and the consequences people actually experience. Its centre is the interaction between direction, alignment, and daily design.

What it maps

Direction, alignment, daily design, habit, environment, repeated choice, values, consequence, and the conversion of intention into lived structure. It keeps the transition between direction and alignment visible, especially under pressure.

Why it matters

A stated value can coexist with systems that pull elsewhere. Looking at daily design reveals the true direction of travel and creates smaller, more actionable points for correction. It helps turn concern about direction and alignment into a more accountable next step.
