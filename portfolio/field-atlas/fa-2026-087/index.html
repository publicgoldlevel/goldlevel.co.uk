THE ARTIFACT AS A RECEIPT OF ATTENTION

FA-2026-087·series·5 ordered frames·No. 02 in fieldPrimary work
Frame 1 of 5

Summary

A reflection on the relationship between creator and creation. The artefact carries traces of attention and interpretation, yet it is neither separate from the observer nor identical with the observer’s whole self. Its centre is the interaction between attention, observer, and selection.

What it maps

Attention, observer, selection, connection, omission, framing, translation, artefact, creator, source object, and the boundary between interpretive movement and external causality. It keeps the transition between attention and observer visible, especially under pressure.

Why it matters

Every visual map contains choices that can disappear behind finished form. Naming the artefact as a receipt makes interpretation visible and preserves a clear proof boundary around what the work can establish. It helps turn concern about attention and observer into a more accountable next step.
