AI MATURITY MUST OUTPACE AI CAPABILITY

FA-2026-069·standalone·1 frame·No. 04 in field
Frame 1 of 1

Summary

A systems overview of technological adolescence and the dependencies surrounding AI. It argues that capability should not outrun governance, technical control, human judgement, economic adaptation, social meaning, or public legitimacy. It places capability, evidence, and governance inside a shared decision field.

What it maps

Capability, evidence, governance, control, human judgement, economic adaptation, public legitimacy, social meaning, dependencies, acceleration, reversibility, and accountability. It includes the feedback loops through which capability changes the practical meaning of evidence.

Why it matters

Capability growth can move risk downstream while institutions remain unprepared. Making maturity a leading requirement shifts attention from what can be built to what can be responsibly held. It supports proportionate action by keeping capability and evidence open to review.
