RELATIONSHIP PRESSURE — SEE BOTH EXPERIENCES

FA-2026-021·standalone·1 frame·No. 28 in field
Frame 1 of 1

Summary

A concise relational field map for moments when pressure narrows the story to one victim and one cause. It invites both experiences into view without erasing impact, responsibility, boundaries, or the need for a clear next action.

What it maps

Pressure, competing experiences, impact, responsibility, blame, agency, and the capacity to hold more than one perspective without collapsing into false equivalence. It includes the feedback loops through which pressure changes the practical meaning of competing experiences.

Why it matters

Seeing both sides is not the same as excusing harm. It can reduce projection, improve repair, and help each person reclaim the part of the situation they can actually choose. It supports proportionate action by keeping pressure and competing experiences open to review.
