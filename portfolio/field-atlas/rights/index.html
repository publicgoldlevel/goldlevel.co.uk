FIELD Atlas

Rights & provenance

FIELD Atlas is published through goldlevel.co.uk. Each work uses a stable identifier in the form FA-2026-NNN, and ordered works retain a numbered frame sequence.

Public metadata

Website-facing files identify GOLDLEVEL and goldlevel.co.uk as the publication context. Private archive paths, account identifiers, source-platform labels, device details, location data, and unrelated export metadata are not part of the public Atlas.

Visual integrity

The intake preserves the staged image payload and frame order. Public filenames are derived from the stable Atlas ID rather than private source identifiers.

Use and permissions

Rights and permissions are administered through GOLDLEVEL unless a work states otherwise. For reproduction, licensing, accessibility files, or provenance questions, use the Goldlevel contact route.

Boundary

FIELD Atlas contains editorial visual essays and conceptual systems maps. Publication does not make a legal, audit, certification, or control-effectiveness claim.
