AI, POWER & THE BETTER OPERATING SETTLEMENT

Summary

A governance field map asking how collective human value becomes private infrastructure and how bargaining power is distributed around AI systems. It rejects both total control and passive acceptance in favour of accountable, revisable terms.

What it maps

Extraction, data and cultural value, leverage asymmetry, concentrated power, compensation, dependency, false polarity, accountability, public value, and operating terms. The sequence also shows where extraction and data and cultural value align, conflict, or require separation.

Why it matters

AI debates can collapse into innovation versus prohibition while ordinary people carry hidden costs. A better settlement makes value, power, exit, responsibility, and burden visible at the edge where systems meet life. The result is a stronger basis for deciding what extraction and data and cultural value require.
