THE RESILIENCE PROTOCOL

FA-2026-067·series·2 ordered frames·No. 13 in field
Frame 1 of 2

Summary

A public-safe visual summary of an externally authored resilience proposal. It focuses on how communities can understand dependencies, preserve knowledge, build alternatives, and maintain continuity when dominant systems fail or must be left. Its practical centre lies where dependency, systemic exit, and resilience requirements meet.

What it maps

Dependency, systemic exit, resilience requirements, knowledge continuity, community capacity, long horizons, source attribution, and the limits of summary. It also attends to the boundaries that determine whether dependency or systemic exit carries authority.

Why it matters

Resilience is weakened when essential knowledge and capability live inside one provider or pathway. Mapping dependencies supports deliberate preparation while preserving the distinction between source material and editorial translation. It creates a clearer route for questioning how dependency and systemic exit are being carried.
