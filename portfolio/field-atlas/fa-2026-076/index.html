CONTEXT LEAKAGE AND HUMAN OVERSIGHT

FA-2026-076·series·3 ordered frames
Frame 1 of 3

Summary

A practical visual note on context leakage between domains. It calls for human oversight throughout the flow so users can see, separate, correct, or stop information moving where it does not belong. The work keeps private and professional context, unrelated data, and connected systems close to consequence.

What it maps

Private and professional context, unrelated data, connected systems, memory, leakage, separation, review, correction, and human oversight across the workflow. The field becomes clearer where private and professional context and unrelated data change pace, role, or consequence.

Why it matters

Context-rich systems can create harm without a dramatic breach; a small piece of misplaced information may change decisions or disclosure. Continuous oversight keeps boundaries meaningful after initial consent. That visibility helps people protect what matters when private and professional context and unrelated data interact.

This is an editorial interpretation of the ordered visual work. It is not legal, clinical, audit, certification, or control-effectiveness advice.
