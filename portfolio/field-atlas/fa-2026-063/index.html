ELECTRONIC POWER AND VISIBLE RESPONSIBILITY

FA-2026-063·standalone·1 frame·No. 15 in field
Frame 1 of 1

Summary

A concise governance statement on systems that influence access to money, work, housing, healthcare, services, reputation, and rights. It insists that responsibility must remain as visible and scalable as the power producing consequences. Its centre is the interaction between electronic systems, public consequence, and chain of custody.

What it maps

Electronic systems, public consequence, chain of custody, explanation, repair, override, appeal, authority, accountability, and stop conditions. It keeps the transition between electronic systems and public consequence visible, especially under pressure.

Why it matters

Power without an accessible responsible party leaves people inside loops they cannot understand or exit. Visible responsibility turns abstract oversight into a practical route for challenge and remedy. It helps turn concern about electronic systems and public consequence into a more accountable next step.
