Stewardship, Boundaries & Source Integrity

CONTROL FREAK OR STEWARD?

FA-2026-048·series·3 ordered frames·No. 13 in field
Frame 1 of 3

Summary

A reflective inquiry into how naming shapes conduct. The work distinguishes a role organised around possession and total control from one organised around care, responsibility, boundaries, and right use. The sequence keeps control, stewardship, and language connected to lived context.

What it maps

Control, stewardship, language, role identity, possession, care, responsibility, autonomy, boundaries, and the different behaviours each frame makes more likely. It also tracks how control and stewardship reinforce or constrain one another over time.

Why it matters

Labels can either shame a person or clarify a practice. The steward frame creates a route towards responsibility without requiring domination, making care more compatible with freedom and correction. That clarity supports more deliberate choices about control and stewardship in practice.
