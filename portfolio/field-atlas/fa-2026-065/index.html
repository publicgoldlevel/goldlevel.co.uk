P30 — INDEPENDENT STEWARDSHIP ASSESSMENT

Summary

A public-facing stewardship assessment of work attributed to an external project. The sequence treats P30 as a proposal to be examined, not an endorsed, affiliated, certified, or completed system. It holds claims, attribution, and independence within one accountable field.

What it maps

Claims, attribution, independence, affiliation, evidence, unknowns, testing, safeguards, adoption, proof gates, public communication, and responsible movement from proposal to implementation. The map keeps the interaction between claims and attribution visible rather than treating either as an isolated variable.

Why it matters

External proposals can gain legitimacy through confident presentation alone. A proof-gated assessment protects creators, reviewers, and affected people by keeping attribution, uncertainty, and required evidence visible. It gives readers a firmer basis for handling claims and attribution responsibly.

This is an editorial interpretation of the ordered visual work. It is not legal, clinical, audit, certification, or control-effectiveness advice.
