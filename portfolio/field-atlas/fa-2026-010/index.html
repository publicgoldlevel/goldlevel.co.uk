Ai Governance And Systems

KEEP FINAL AUTHORITY HUMAN

FA-2026-010·series·3 ordered frames
Frame 1 of 3

Summary

A three-frame governance note on using AI without surrendering decision authority. It joins portability, exit, context control, and human judgement into one operational requirement rather than treating them as separate preferences. The framing keeps tool use, context portability, and exit rights visible at the same time.

What it maps

Tool use, context portability, exit rights, dependency, consequential decisions, and the point at which assistance can quietly become authority. It follows the points where tool use and context portability meet, diverge, or alter practical choice.

Why it matters

Human oversight is weak when people cannot leave, move their context, or understand what shaped an output. Keeping these conditions together makes authority more than a ceremonial final click. The distinction makes decisions about tool use and context portability easier to examine and revise.

This is an editorial interpretation of the ordered visual work. It is not legal, clinical, audit, certification, or control-effectiveness advice.
