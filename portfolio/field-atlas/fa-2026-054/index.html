Stewardship, Boundaries & Source Integrity

DO NOT LET YOUR CREATIONS BECOME YOU

FA-2026-054·series·6 ordered frames·No. 12 in field
Frame 1 of 6

Summary

A stewardship sequence on the relationship between maker and made. Creations can clarify, reflect, and serve, but they do not become the whole world, the author’s identity, or a substitute for body, conscience, freedom, and relation.

What it maps

Creator, artefact, identity, machine, judgement, conscience, embodiment, freedom, service, and the boundary between being shaped by work and being possessed by it. The sequence also shows where creator and artefact align, conflict, or require separation.

Why it matters

Creators can become over-identified with systems that reward attention and repetition. Maintaining separation protects correction, wellbeing, ethical responsibility, and the ability to stop or change the work. The result is a stronger basis for deciding what creator and artefact require.
