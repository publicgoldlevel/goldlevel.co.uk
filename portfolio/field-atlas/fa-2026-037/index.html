CONTEXT CAPACITY — NOTICE, NAME, PLACE, BOUND, ACT

FA-2026-037·series·4 ordered frames·No. 11 in field
Frame 1 of 4

Summary

A practical sequence joining facts, role, capacity, evidence, and boundaries before movement. Notice, name, place, bound, act, review, and refine become a repeatable method for avoiding both blind reaction and over-carrying. It places context, role, and capacity inside a shared decision field.

What it maps

Context, role, capacity, evidence, boundaries, proportionate action, review, refinement, cooperation, and the placement of responsibility where it can actually be held. It includes the feedback loops through which context changes the practical meaning of role.

Why it matters

Many conflicts worsen because one person or conversation is asked to hold the whole system. Context capacity creates cleaner choices, more realistic cooperation, and action that does not require anyone to disappear. It supports proportionate action by keeping context and role open to review.
