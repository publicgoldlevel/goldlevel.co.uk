ADHD — BUILD A VESSEL FOR THE SIGNAL

FA-2026-004·series·6 ordered frames·No. 02 in fieldPrimary work
Frame 1 of 6

Summary

An experiential map of ADHD-related attention as a powerful but uneven signal rather than a simple absence of focus. The work asks how routines, environments, boundaries, and practical containers can support that signal without extinguishing it.

What it maps

Meaning-led attention, urgency, energy, structure, environmental support, self-knowledge, and the distinction between suppressing intensity and giving it a workable vessel. The field becomes clearer where meaning-led attention and urgency change pace, role, or consequence.

Why it matters

A person may not need less vitality; they may need conditions that make vitality usable. The map offers a humane design question while remaining reflective rather than diagnostic or prescriptive. That visibility helps people protect what matters when meaning-led attention and urgency interact.
