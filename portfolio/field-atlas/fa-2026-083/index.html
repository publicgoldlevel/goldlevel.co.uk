SEE THE SWITCH

FA-2026-083·series·14 ordered frames·No. 01 in fieldPrimary work
Frame 1 of 14

Summary

A visual sequence on learning to see the operations beneath a working system. Once mechanism and function are visible, the question shifts from whether an output is convenient to how participation, benefit, cost, and authority are arranged.

What it maps

Mechanism, function, switch, participation, benefit, cost, output, understanding, convenience, governance, neutrality, and the user’s role inside the operating system. It also attends to the boundaries that determine whether mechanism or function carries authority.

Why it matters

Systems can govern behaviour without appearing political because they simply work. Mechanism literacy helps people recognise the terms of participation and choose more deliberately where power is being carried. It creates a clearer route for questioning how mechanism and function are being carried.
