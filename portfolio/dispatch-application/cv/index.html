Profile

Writing, attention and reliable follow-through.

I write, edit and build work for readers. My independent practice is supported by experience in clinical placements, research, client design, retail and fulfilment.

Across those settings I learned to keep clear records, listen, explain, revise, prioritise and carry work through. I bring those habits forward without asking earlier roles to define the work I am making now.
