Why I’m applying

I want to bring this practice into local reporting.

I direct each project from the first question to the final page—writing, editing, sequencing and building the public presentation myself.

I do not yet have three years in a newsroom. I can show the work, the care behind it and a willingness to be tested, edited and improved.

I want to report Birmingham by staying close to people, places, records and decisions.
