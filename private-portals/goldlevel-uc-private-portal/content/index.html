SAR. UK GDPR Articles 12 and 15 and the ICO's current subject-access guidance require a response without undue delay, ordinarily within one month, subject to lawful timing provisions. Data-protection complaint. DPA 2018 section 164A, inserted by DUAA 2025 section 103 and in force from 19 June 2026, requires acknowledgment within 30 days of receipt and appropriate response steps, progress information and the outcome without undue delay. Privacy and accuracy. UK GDPR Article 5 requires, among other things, relevant and limited processing, accuracy, appropriate retention and security, and accountability. Fair participation and challenge. Osborn v Parole Board [2013] UKSC 61, paragraphs 67-71, explains the value of relevant information, participation and respect in administrative fairness.
