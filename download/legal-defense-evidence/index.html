Counsel-triage brief

LEGAL DEFENSE EVIDENCE

A bounded evidence-prioritisation brief for counsel review: what to preserve first, what remains unproven, and which records would materially strengthen or weaken the narrative.

Status before download

Internal counsel-review draft. Not legal advice. Privilege, work-product, admissibility, disclosure, preservation duties, and jurisdictional treatment must be determined by qualified counsel.

Purpose

Separate occurrence from explanation.

The brief does not treat timing, repetition, multiple institutions, or a coherent story as proof of common control. It distinguishes the event record from attribution, motive, coordination, and damages.

Highest-value attributes

Built for triage, not spectacle.

The six-page craft expands the original one-page source into a readable sequence while preserving its evidential limits.

01

Eight evidence packets

Contracts, platform and bank records, court files, provider notices, funding records, settlement terms, and authoritative chronology.

02

24-vector crosswalk

Each vector retains its mechanism, sector, evidence grade, priority, next receipt, and current state.

03

Counterevidence preserved

Alternative explanations and disconfirming material remain part of the evidence file rather than being edited out.

04

Promotion gates

Physical-harm and common-control hypotheses remain held unless direct corroboration supports promotion.

Evidence sequence

Preserve before interpreting.

  1. Freeze native originals and primary records.
  2. Record SHA-256, metadata, custodian, source, and acquisition context.
  3. Preserve exports, attachments, and adverse or disconfirming material.
  4. Build an independently timestamped chronology.
  5. Separate occurrence, attribution, motive, coordination, and damages.
  6. Map actor independence, ownership, funding, instruction, and decision control.
  7. Test alternative explanations symmetrically.
  8. Let counsel determine admissibility, disclosure, privilege, hold, and jurisdiction.
